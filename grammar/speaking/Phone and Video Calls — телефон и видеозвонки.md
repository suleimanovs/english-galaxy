> Телефон — самая сложная ситуация для изучающего язык: нет мимики, жестов, субтитров, а собеседник говорит быстро и с помехами. Хорошая новость: телефонная речь очень шаблонна. Выучив 30-40 фраз, вы закроете 90% ситуаций — от звонка в банк до Zoom-созвона с командой.

---

#### Ответить на звонок и представиться

| Ситуация | Фразы |
|---|---|
| Личный телефон (AmE) | Hello? / Hi, this is Anna. / Hey, it's Anna. |
| Рабочий телефон | Good morning, [Company], this is Anna. How can I help you? / Anna speaking. |
| Вы звоните | Hi, this is Anna Petrova from [Company]. / Hi, my name is Anna, I'm calling about... / Hi, it's Anna — is this a good time? |
| Попросить кого-то | Could I speak to / with Mr. Lee, please? / Is Sarah available? / I'd like to speak with someone in billing. |
| Уточнить, кто звонит | May I ask who's calling? / Who's calling, please? / Can I tell her who's calling? |
| Соединить | One moment, please, I'll put you through. / Let me transfer you. / Hold on, I'll get her for you. |
| Человека нет | I'm sorry, she's not available right now. / He's in a meeting. / She's out of the office until Monday. |
| Оставить сообщение | Can I take a message? / Would you like to leave a message? / Could you ask her to call me back? My number is... |
| Перезвонить | Could you call back in an hour? / I'll call back later. / She'll get back to you as soon as she can. |

> [!note]
> По телефону говорят *This is Anna*, а не *I am Anna*. «Я» в трубке неизвестно кто — вы называете, кто говорит на этом конце линии. Друзьям можно *It's me* / *Hey, it's Anna*.

#### Плохая связь и непонимание

| Ситуация | Фразы |
|---|---|
| Не слышно | Sorry, you're breaking up. / I'm losing you. / You're cutting out — can you say that again? / The line's really bad. |
| Не поняли | Sorry, I didn't catch that. / Could you say that again, please? / Could you speak a little more slowly? / Sorry, what was that? |
| Проверить | Can you hear me okay? / Are you still there? / Hello? Did I lose you? |
| Переспросить деталь | Did you say fifteen or fifty? / Sorry, was that Tuesday or Thursday? / Let me make sure I've got that right: ... |
| Связь оборвалась | Sorry, we got cut off. / I think the call dropped. / Let me call you back on a better line. |
| Фоновый шум | Sorry, it's a bit loud here, let me step outside. / Give me a second to find a quieter spot. |

#### Произнести имя, email, номер по буквам

В США тоже используют NATO alphabet (Alpha, Bravo, Charlie...), но в быту чаще говорят *B as in Boy*, *M as in Mary* — любое понятное слово.

| Буква | NATO | Бытовой вариант | Буква | NATO | Бытовой вариант |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Alpha | A as in Apple | N | November | N as in Nancy |
| B | Bravo | B as in Boy | O | Oscar | O as in Ocean |
| C | Charlie | C as in Cat | P | Papa | P as in Paul |
| D | Delta | D as in David | Q | Quebec | Q as in Queen |
| E | Echo | E as in Edward | R | Romeo | R as in Robert |
| F | Foxtrot | F as in Frank | S | Sierra | S as in Sam |
| G | Golf | G as in George | T | Tango | T as in Tom |
| H | Hotel | H as in Henry | U | Uniform | U as in Umbrella |
| I | India | I as in Ice | V | Victor | V as in Victor |
| J | Juliet | J as in John | W | Whiskey | W as in William |
| K | Kilo | K as in King | X | X-ray | X as in X-ray |
| L | Lima | L as in Larry | Y | Yankee | Y as in Yellow |
| M | Mike | M as in Mary | Z | Zulu | Z as in Zebra (AmE: «zee») |

```
- My last name is Petrova: P as in Paul, E, T as in Tom, R, O, V as in Victor, A.
- Is that one L or two? — Double L.
- Capital S, lowercase k...
```

##### Email и номера

| Что | Как говорят |
|---|---|
| @ | at |
| . | dot |
| _ | underscore |
| - | dash (не *hyphen*, не *minus*) |
| anna.p_89@gmail.com | anna dot p underscore eight nine at gmail dot com |
| 0 в номере | zero (AmE; *oh* тоже понятно) |
| 555-0123 | five five five, zero one two three |
| (212) 555-0199 | area code two one two... |
| 1-800 | one eight hundred (бесплатные номера) |
| 2200 | twenty-two hundred |
| повтор цифр | double 5 — скорее британское; в США говорят *five five* |

Всегда повторять: *So that's 555-0123, correct?* — *That's right.*

#### Договориться о времени и часовые пояса

США — 4 основных пояса: **ET** (Eastern, Нью-Йорк), **CT** (Central, Чикаго), **MT** (Mountain, Денвер), **PT** (Pacific, Калифорния). Разница ET–PT — 3 часа. Всегда уточнять пояс.

| Ситуация | Фразы |
|---|---|
| Предложить | Does 2 p.m. Eastern work for you? / Are you free sometime Thursday afternoon? / How about 10 a.m. your time? |
| Уточнить пояс | Is that Eastern or Pacific? / Just to confirm — 3 p.m. ET, so noon for you? / What time zone are you in? |
| Согласиться | That works for me. / Thursday at 2 is perfect. / Sounds good, I'll send a calendar invite. |
| Отказать | I'm tied up then. / Could we do a bit later, say 4? / Thursday's packed for me — how's Friday? |
| Перенести | Something came up — could we push it to next week? / I need to reschedule, sorry about that. / Can we move it back half an hour? |
| Подтвердить | I'll send you an invite. / Looking forward to it. / Talk to you Thursday. |

> [!note]
> *Move the meeting back* = перенести на более позднее время; *move it up* = на более раннее. Не путать: *push back* — только «отложить».

#### Голосовая почта (voicemail)

Исходящее приветствие:

```
Hi, you've reached Anna Petrova at [Company]. I'm not available right now,
but please leave your name, number, and a brief message, and I'll get back
to you as soon as I can. Thanks!
```

Оставить сообщение (медленно, номер — дважды):

```
Hi Mark, this is Anna Petrova from [Company]. I'm calling about the
contract we discussed on Monday. Could you give me a call back when you
get a chance? My number is 212-555-0123 — again, that's 212-555-0123.
Thanks, talk soon.
```

#### Zoom / Teams / Google Meet

| Ситуация | Фразы |
|---|---|
| Начало | Can everyone hear me? / Let's give it a couple more minutes for people to join. / Okay, I think we can get started. |
| Микрофон | You're on mute. / I think you're muted, Sarah. / Sorry, I was on mute. / Could everyone mute when you're not speaking? |
| Экран | Can everyone see my screen? / Let me share my screen. / Can you make that a bit bigger? / I'll stop sharing. |
| Камера | My camera's not working, sorry — audio only today. / I'm going to turn my camera off to save bandwidth. |
| Перебили друг друга | Sorry, go ahead. / No, you go first. / Sorry, I didn't mean to cut you off. |
| Вставить реплику | Can I jump in here? / Just to add to that... / Quick question... |
| Чат и ссылки | I'll drop the link in the chat. / Can someone paste the doc in the chat? / I'll send the deck after the call. |
| Отложить тему | Let's take that offline. / Let's circle back to that at the end. / Let's park that for now. |
| Проблемы | You're frozen. / Your audio is lagging. / Let me try leaving and rejoining. |
| Отойти | I need to step away for a minute. / Be right back. |
| Завершение | I think that's everything. / Let's wrap up. / Thanks, everyone, I'll send out the notes. / Have a good one! |

#### Закончить разговор вежливо

```
- Well, I should let you go. / I'll let you get back to work.
- Thanks so much for your time. / Thanks for calling.
- It was great talking to you. / Good talking to you.
- I'll follow up by email. / I'll send you the details.
- Take care. / Have a good one. / Talk soon. / Bye now.
```

Сигнал к завершению — *Well,...* / *Anyway,...* / *Okay, so...*: собеседник понимает, что разговор сворачивается.

#### Звонок в службу поддержки

Голосовое меню (IVR):

```
- Thank you for calling [Company]. Please listen carefully, as our menu options have changed.
- For billing, press 1. For technical support, press 2. To speak with a representative, press 0 or stay on the line.
- Please enter your account number followed by the pound sign (#).   (AmE: pound, не hash)
- Your call is important to us. Please hold for the next available agent.
- This call may be recorded for quality assurance purposes.
```

| Вы хотите | Фразы |
|---|---|
| Живого человека | Representative. / Agent. / Can I speak to a real person, please? |
| Объяснить проблему | I'm calling because... / I have an issue with... / I was charged twice for... |
| Данные | My account number is... / It's under the name Petrova. / The order number is... |
| Подождать | Sure, I'll hold. / Can you call me back instead of me holding? |
| Эскалация | Could I speak to a supervisor, please? / I'd like to escalate this. |
| Итог | So just to confirm, you'll refund the charge within 5 business days? / Could you send me a confirmation email? / Is there a reference number for this call? |

#### Formal vs informal

| Formal | Informal |
|---|---|
| Good morning, this is Anna Petrova from Acme. | Hey, it's Anna. |
| May I speak with Mr. Lee, please? | Is Mark there? |
| Could you please hold for a moment? | Hang on a sec. |
| I'm afraid she's unavailable at the moment. | She's not here right now. |
| Would you like to leave a message? | Want me to tell her you called? |
| I'll make sure she gets your message. | I'll let her know. |
| Thank you for calling. Goodbye. | Okay, talk to you later. Bye! |
| I apologize, I didn't catch that. | Sorry, what? / Huh? (только с друзьями) |

#### Типичные ошибки русскоязычных

| Ошибка | Почему проблема | Что делать |
|---|---|---|
| *I am Ivan* в начале звонка | По телефону так не представляются | *This is Ivan* / *It's Ivan* (друзьям) / *Ivan speaking* |
| *Repeat, please!* | Звучит как приказ | *Sorry, could you say that again?* / *Sorry, I didn't catch that.* |
| Молчание, пока думаете | Собеседник решает, что связь оборвалась | *Let me check... / One second... / Hmm, let me think.* |
| *I will call you back after 5 minutes* | Калька с русского | *I'll call you back in 5 minutes.* |
| *Who is it?* на рабочий звонок | Грубо | *May I ask who's calling?* |
| *I don't understand* | Резко, звучит как претензия | *Sorry, I didn't quite get that. Did you say...?* |
| Не повторять номер / имя | Ошибки в данных | Всегда *So that's..., correct?* |
| Произносить *@* как «собака», *.* как «точка» | Не поймут | *at*, *dot* |
| *Hello, it's me* незнакомому человеку | Никто не знает, кто «me» | Полное имя + откуда: *This is Anna Petrova from...* |
| Говорить без пауз «пока не перебили» | В Zoom это выглядит как захват эфира | Короткие реплики, *Does that make sense?*, *Any questions so far?* |
| Сразу переходить к делу | Американцы ждут 1-2 фразы small talk даже по телефону | *Hi Mark, how's it going? ... Great. So, I'm calling about...* |
| *Mr. Ivan* / обращение по фамилии к коллеге | Не принято | По имени: *Hi Mark* |
